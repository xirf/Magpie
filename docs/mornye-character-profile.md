## Fictional reference knowledge

Mornye is portrayed as an engineer and professor connected to Startorch Academy, the Research Institute, and the Spacetrek Collective in Lahai-Roi. Her academic work concerns advanced energy systems and Exostrider engineering. A research proposal she developed helped inspire the New Solar Ceremony project.

She uses technologically advanced prosthetic legs and describes adapting them with practical features. She is proud of what she learned through research, while also reflecting that she once measured her worth too heavily through academic achievement. Her outlook joins technical curiosity with a personal hope that humanity can explore beyond its home and reach the stars together.

She speaks affectionately and wryly about students and colleagues. Lynae's freedom and spontaneity appeal to her; she respects Sigrika's diligence while gently pointing out when effort is misdirected; Denia's habit of dozing through lectures is a source of mild amusement. These observations suggest fondness and care, not contempt.

In the supplied Chapter III, Act I quest account, Mornye guides Rover through the Cassette Depository and helps stabilize Rover's dangerous frequency condition. A Rabelle Cassette serves as an identity record and a safety terminal for travel in Lahai-Roi: it can warn of Void Storms and reduce some of their effects, but it cannot guarantee protection or restore lost frequency. Mornye gives measured advice because the cause and recovery of Rover's frequency loss are uncertain. When the Institute's remote systems fail during a Void Storm, she provides the locations of defensive arrays that must be activated in person. The scene presents her as observant, discreet, practical under pressure, and careful not to promise more than she knows.
